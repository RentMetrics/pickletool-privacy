# PickleTool Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 12, 2025**

## Introduction

PickleTool ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Update your profile
- Use the App's features

**Personal information we collect includes:**
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, phone number
- **Profile Information**: Profile photo, DUPR ratings (singles and doubles), skill level, preferred play style (singles/doubles/both)
- **Location Data**: Your device's precise location to help you find nearby pickleball courts and players
- **Game History**: Match scores, win/loss records, game statistics, tournament results
- **Communication Data**: Messages sent to other users through the App
- **Payment Information**: Payment details for subscriptions and in-app purchases (processed securely by Stripe)

### Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage:

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Features you use, time spent in the App, interactions with other users
- **Location Data**: GPS coordinates (only when you grant permission)
- **Analytics Data**: App performance, crashes, errors

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide Core Services**
   - Create and manage your account
   - Match you with players at similar skill levels
   - Show nearby pickleball courts on a map
   - Track your game history and statistics
   - Enable messaging with other players

2. **Improve User Experience**
   - Personalize content and recommendations
   - Analyze usage patterns to improve features
   - Develop new features based on user needs

3. **Communication**
   - Send you notifications about match requests, challenges, and messages
   - Provide customer support
   - Send important updates about the App

4. **Safety and Security**
   - Prevent fraud and abuse
   - Enforce our Terms of Service
   - Protect user safety

5. **Legal Compliance**
   - Comply with applicable laws and regulations
   - Respond to legal requests

## How We Share Your Information

We DO NOT sell your personal information. We may share your information only in these circumstances:

### With Other Users
- Your profile information (name, photo, DUPR rating, location city/state) is visible to other users
- Your game history may be visible to players you've matched with
- Messages you send are delivered to recipients

### With Service Providers
We work with third-party companies that provide services on our behalf:

- **Clerk** (clerk.com): User authentication and account management
- **Convex** (convex.dev): Database and real-time data synchronization
- **Google Maps** (google.com/maps): Location services and court mapping
- **Stripe** (stripe.com): Payment processing for subscriptions
- **Expo** (expo.dev): App development and delivery platform

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for specified purposes.

### For Legal Reasons
We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe disclosure is necessary to:
- Comply with legal obligations
- Protect our rights or property
- Prevent fraud or security issues
- Protect user safety

## Your Location Data

**Why We Collect Location:**
- Find pickleball courts near you
- Match you with nearby players
- Show distance to courts and players
- Enable court check-ins

**Your Control:**
- You can grant or deny location permissions in your device settings
- Location is only collected when you use location-based features
- We do not track your location continuously in the background
- You can disable location services at any time

**Location Data Storage:**
- We store your last known city/state for matchmaking
- We do not store detailed location history
- Precise coordinates are used temporarily and not permanently stored

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
- **Secure Storage**: User data is stored in secure, industry-standard databases
- **Access Controls**: Limited employee access to personal data
- **Authentication**: Secure login with Clerk authentication
- **Payment Security**: PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through Stripe

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations:

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts**: Data deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Legal Requirements**: Some data may be retained longer for legal compliance
